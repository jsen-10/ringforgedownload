04 FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. RingForge Studio is a free, fan-made tool.

Does it come with any games?

No. RingForge Studio doesn't include any ROMs. Use a backup of a game you own.

Will it break my ROM?

Always keep a backup of your original ROM. Edit a copy, and you can always start fresh.

How do I share my hack?

Share a patch file (like IPS or BPS) made from your original and edited ROMs, never the ROM itself.
